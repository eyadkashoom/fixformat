The recovery pass walks the volume in fixed slices and keeps only the regions whose byte statistics look like language. Every candidate is then scored against a trigram model, and the survivors are written to the report with their offsets and their declared encoding.
A disk image is not a document: it holds deleted pages, compressed archives, executables and uninitialised space side by side. The filter must therefore reject binary noise without discarding real text that happens to sit next to it.
Operators care about three numbers: how much text was found, how much of it is real, and how long the scan took. A fast scanner that invents text is worse than a slow one that does not.
